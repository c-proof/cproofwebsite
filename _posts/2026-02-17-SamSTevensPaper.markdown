We have been paralyzed by changes in the social media landscape, and have hence been very remiss in recognizing a very nice paper by Sam Stevens and Wiley Evans at Hakai Institute, and strongly inspired by questions being asked by Charles Hannah at DFO.  They show a definite decline in oxygen levels on the shelf north of Vancouver Island.  [Read the paper here](https://agupubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/epdf/10.1029/2025GB008608), and see the media coverage at [Tula magazine](https://tula.org/news/hypoxia-emerges-as-a-new-threat-on-canadas-pacific-shelf) and [CBC (youtube)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ENc_jcgyyes).

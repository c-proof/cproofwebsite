We had an excellent time at the [Ocean Sciences Meeting 2026](https://www.agu.org/ocean-sciences-meeting) in Glasgow, Scotland, where C-PROOF was well represented by posters and talks.  Giuliana Berden presented a poster on the C-PROOF observations of a California Undercurrent Eddy ("Cuddy"), and Lauryn Talbot presented a talk on the C-PROOF observations about the variability along LineP in the ocean spice section.  We had a great time catching up with old friends and making new ones, and we are looking forward to the next OSM in 2028 in Vancouver B.C.!

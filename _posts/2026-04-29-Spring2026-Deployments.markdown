C-PROOF has been busy deploying gliders, with a recent turn around of `dfo-bumblebee998` by the Hakai crew at Calvert Island.  `dfo-hal1002` is doing the Southern Line which has now passed two years of almost continuous occupation.  We have also started running gliders off the west coast of Vancouver Island as part of a coordinated effort with the PRODIGY field school, led by UBC.  `dfo-mike579` is a turbulence glider that is sitting in a basin just offshore of Barkley Sound, while `dfo-eva025` is doing a box transect around `dfo-mike579`. This is work being led by PRODIGY post-doc Shannon McLish. Thanks to Bamfield Marine Science Centre for their support deploying these gliders!  We will be also taking the _CCGS Vector_  to sample both Barkley Sound and the Vancouver Island shelf next week.  When `dfo-mike579` runs low on batteries, he will be replaced by `dfo-kitt1049` a turbulence glider we haven't worked with very much yet, so fingers crossed that goes well.
